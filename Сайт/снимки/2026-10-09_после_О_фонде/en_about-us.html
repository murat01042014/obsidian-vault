About us

The Central Asia Climate Foundation (the Project Office for Central Asia on Climate Change and Green Energy) was established on 22 February 2024 in implementation of the initiative of the President of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, announced at the 78th session of the UN General Assembly in 2023.

The Foundation is a private charitable organization registered in the Astana International Financial Centre and operating under the current law of the AIFC as well as the national legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

The Central Asia Climate Foundation is a legal entity with legal personality separate from its founder and any other person, and is guided by the objectives set out in its Charter.

The Foundation's main sources of funding are charitable and sponsorship assistance, grants from international organizations and development institutions, and funds received from its charter activities.

Our mission

Accelerating the climate transition in Central Asia through support for policies, innovations and partnerships.

Promoting the sustainable development of Central Asia by uniting the efforts of states, international organizations, business and the expert community to implement projects and initiatives in the areas of climate change, green energy, environmental protection and biodiversity conservation.

Operations

In accordance with paragraph 24 of the Declaration of the Heads of State of Central Asia "Environmental Solidarity of Central Asia", adopted at the Regional Ecological Summit held in Astana on 22–24 April 2026, the presidents expressed their readiness to cooperate within the Project Office for Central Asia on Climate Change and Green Energy in order to accelerate regional action aimed at promoting the ecological transition in the region.

The Foundation's activities are aimed at building, on its basis, a sustainable regional Central Asian platform for developing, supporting and implementing environmental, climate and energy initiatives, drawing on the potential of the Project Office, international partnerships and the outcomes of the Regional Ecological Summit.

• support for policy development and regional cooperation, including a just transition;

• analytical and expert work: research on climate policy, carbon regulation and green finance; assessment of climate and environmental risks; expert review of projects and programs;

• regional climate and environmental projects and initiatives, including: organization of international, regional and national summits, conferences and forums, etc.;

• ESG: methodological support, assessment, consulting and training for organizations on sustainable development and non-financial disclosure;

• climate finance and carbon markets: mobilization of climate, grant and project financing, offset projects; grant support for environmental and climate projects;

• environmental protection: waste management, air and water pollution, land degradation and desertification; participation in the Presidential initiative “Taza Kazakhstan”;

• digital solutions: tools and platforms based on artificial intelligence and geographic information systems for collecting, analyzing and monitoring environmental and climate data;

• education and youth engagement: training and capacity building for specialists, “Green Schools”, volunteering, environmental education;

• promotion of environmental initiatives of government bodies, business, civil society and youth;

• partnerships with international organizations, financial institutions, business and the expert community.

Our mission

Accelerating the climate transition in Central Asia by supporting policies, innovations, and partnerships.

We aim to:

Build partnerships to maximize resources for climate action

Actively participate in the formation and implementation of policies that promote environmental and climate sustainability

Support and develop cooperation among Central Asian countries in addressing climate challenges

Stimulate research and innovation in the field of clean technologies and climate sustainability

Our Objectives

Accelerating regional climate action

 

Support and implement projects, programs, and initiatives to facilitate climate transition and action, promote best practices for a green economy, and support ecological transition and sustainable development in Central Asia

Enhancing regional climate policy and cooperation

 

Support the development of climate policies in the region, fostering cooperation among Central Asian countries to address shared climate challenges and opportunities

Supporting policy development

 

Assist in the development, advocacy, and implementation of policies and regulations that promote environmental sustainability and climate resilience in collaboration with stakeholders

Fostering research and innovation

Encourage and finance research and innovation in clean technologies, climate resilience and transition, and sustainable agriculture practices to create locally adapted solutions for Central Asia's unique climate challenges

Cultivating partnerships and collective action

Stablish partnerships with international financial institutions, civil society organizations, private sector entities, and research institutions to leverage additional expertise, resources, and funding for climate action in Central Asia through the Foundation

Strengthening climate data and science

Support research, data collection, and analysis on climate patterns, impacts, and solutions, facilitating evidence-based policy making and project implementation in Central Asia

Raising awareness and capacity development

Develop the capacity of stakeholders on climate change, promote awareness of the population on challenges posed by climate change and environmental degradation, and encourage knowledge sharing in addressing those challenges in the region
